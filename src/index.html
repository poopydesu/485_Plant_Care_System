Plant Care System

Welcome to our plant care web-application! We host a variety of information that pertains to the health and well-being of your plants. You will find an assortment of useful tools to learn, log, and explore the different attributes that your plants have.

Login

We reccommend that you create an account with us to begin logging everything about your own plants! To sign up, just click "login" in the navigation bar and follow the prompts.

View Plants

Viewing information about your plants and exploring new options is incredibly easy, just click "view plants" in the navigation bar, find the name of your desired plant, and read all about it!

About Us

Want to learn more about the development, sources, or code? Click on "about us" in the navigation bar to learn more!
